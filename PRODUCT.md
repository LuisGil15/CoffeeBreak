# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People preparing coffee at home who want clear recipes, guided preparation, and a useful recipe book for different tastes and methods. This audience description is confirmed by Luis.

## Product Purpose

CoffeeBreak calculates coffee recipes from the selected brewing method, strength, drink type, and serving size, then guides the user through preparation. It should also work as a visual recipe book with useful preparation options for a broad range of coffee drinkers. Success means users can find a preparation, understand its quantities, and follow its steps on a phone.

## Positioning

No distinct competitive positioning is established in the current product materials.

## Operating Context

The app is a public web tool used while setting up and preparing coffee on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers, with mobile-first design. It has no accounts or user profiles. Users can change preparation settings, follow recipe steps, save favorites, and revisit recent recipes in the browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current recipes cover Chemex, V60, French Press, and AeroPress; Moka and Espresso are marked as not implemented.
- Users can adjust intensity, beverage, number of cups, and cup volume.
- Recipes include coffee, water, optional milk or ice, and method-specific instructions.
- Favorites and history are stored locally in the browser.
- Cold Brew offers concentrate (1:8 balanced recipe, dilute 1:1 when serving) and ready-to-drink (1:15 balanced recipe) modes, with a coarse grind and an informational 14-hour refrigerated infusion. Its step guide advances manually; it does not run a countdown or alerts.
- The recipe book includes one-cup café drink recipes for cappuccino, latte, mocha, and chai latte, with measured ingredients, manual steps, and links to the reference recipes.
- Drink recipes use a staged cup illustration to explain their ingredients; motion is brief and disabled for reduced-motion preferences.
- The same responsive web experience should work across screen sizes, with the phone as the primary design target.

## Brand Commitments

The existing product name is CoffeeBreak. The visual direction should feel like a modern, welcoming café while keeping preparation controls and calculated recipes in the foreground.

## Evidence on Hand

The current React and TypeScript application, method recipe data, and product README. No customer proof, testimonials, or measured performance claims are present.

## Product Principles

- Make recipe quantities understandable at a glance.
- Guide preparation with clear, method-specific steps.
- Keep the workflow usable on a phone.
- Let users return to recipes they use often.
- Use warm café cues without turning the preparation flow into a catalog or magazine.
- Do not introduce account or profile UI.
