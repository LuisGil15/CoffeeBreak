## Scope
CoffeeBreak’s responsive coffee recipe calculator and guided preparation flow.
Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job
Home coffee drinkers choose a method and drink size, read exact amounts, and follow preparation steps. Keep the existing recipe methods, intensity, beverage, amount, local favorites and history. Add Cold Brew with concentrate and ready-to-drink options. The site is public and has no accounts or profiles. Its mobile web layout is primary.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One preparation workspace turns a few familiar choices into exact amounts and a followable recipe. The home screen is the tool itself, not a campaign or recipe gallery.
OWN-WORLD: Use the selected café references: espresso-brown header, crema surfaces, copper for primary actions and progress, and a restrained sage note color. Pair warm serif display and measured recipe numerals with readable sans-serif controls. The signature move is the four-measure recipe panel: coffee, water, ratio and duration always share one scan line or compact two-by-two grid.
STORY: Choose method, drink style and serving amount; see the recipe update immediately; enter guided steps and advance manually. Durations remain informational. Favorites and history stay in this browser.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep a compact brand bar and Preparar / Recetario / Guardadas navigation. On mobile, show the selected method, compact Cold Brew modes, serving controls and the four-measure result with a full-width “Empezar preparación” action. On wide screens, place controls left and the live recipe right. The separate guided view uses a step count, instruction, static duration, ingredient recap and previous / next controls; no live countdown or alerts.
FORM: Operate tool workspace, based on the user-approved calculator and guided-preparation attachments. The first assigned seed was index 5 of 7, key fd2fc3ce; the user’s selected visual direction overrides it.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
